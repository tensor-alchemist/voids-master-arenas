How to use this tool The campaign card lists an In-game passcode for every round — click one to copy, then paste it into the Ripatorium's passcode field and the game sets every demon slider for that round automatically (we reverse-engineered id Software's passcode format to make this work). Global Settings and Wave Respawns still need entering by hand — they aren't part of id's passcode — or hit Copy Setup for a plain-text checklist. Every number this tool produces already respects the exact min/max/step limits of the in-game sliders.

Pick an intensity: Normal for a solid fight with heavies pushing through the chaff, Praetor for superheavies from the first round, a climb that never lets up, and a full kit (Health & Ammo always respawning, BFC and .93 Shotgun maxed), or Slaughterhouse for nothing but the great beasts and no powerups. Flip on Slaughter Mode for a fixed five-round descent through every circle, ending in a boss finale on a 20-minute clock.

Choose how many Rounds you want (or leave it on Random), and if you don't own the DLC, switch Content Pool to Base Game Only. The time limit is sized automatically from an estimate of how long the fight takes, with no slack to spare. Every setting on the campaign card (arena, time, Health & Ammo, Berserk and the rest) can be changed after the roll.

Your settings and last campaign are remembered between visits, recent rolls are kept under Previous Hordes, and pressing R rerolls the horde. Rolled something brutal? Copy Link grabs a shareable URL — anyone opening it gets your exact campaign, passcode and all.
